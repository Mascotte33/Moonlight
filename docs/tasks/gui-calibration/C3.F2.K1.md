# C3.F2.K1: Battle list uses the scale

Part of [gui-calibration](../../prs/gui-calibration.md).

## Brief

Multiply the battle constants by `profile.ui_scale` when building `BattleReader`. Don't change the constants' values.

**Done when:** `pytest tests/unit/test_battle.py` passes; a battle list crop enlarged 1.5× with `ui_scale` 1.5 reads the same creatures and HP as at 1.0.

## Router notes

2026-10-22:
- Added `moonlight/setup/autofind.py`, `tests/unit/test_autofind.py` and `tests/golden_profile/moonlight_user.json` to `writes`. The reader found no creatures in the Battle List box from C2.F2.K3, because the reader's box starts `TOP_PADDING` (11 px) above the first creature row and the finder's box started at that row. Fixed in the finder, since the reader's constants stay as they are.

## What changed and why

- The battle list finder now returns the reader's box, 11 px higher and 11 px taller. On `tests/golden/battle/full.png` that is (2212, 22, 156, 484). The golden profile's `battle_list` goes back to [2388, 597, 156, 49], which matches the old hand-drawn y of 597.
- `BattleReader` multiplies its pixel sizes by `profile.ui_scale` once, in `__init__`. That covers slot height, top padding, content width, name area, HP bar rows and run, and the 20 px target frame. The module constants keep their values.
- A 1 px line covers up to `ceil(ui_scale)` px once enlarged. So the HP fill row, the fill's inner margin and the target frame's top and bottom rows look across that many px. At 1.0 this is exactly the old rule.
- Test: the 12 creatures in `full.png` read the same at 1.5x with nearest-neighbour enlarging, including the selected one and 100 % HP. With smooth enlarging, creatures and HP still match, but the 1 px red target frame blurs to 213,0,0, outside the red tolerance of 30. A real client at 1.5x may draw it either way. Only a live check can tell (owner check).
