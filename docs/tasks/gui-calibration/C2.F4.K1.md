# C2.F4.K1: Calibration steps (no screen yet)

Part of [gui-calibration](../../prs/gui-calibration.md).

## Brief

A plain class that walks the 7 areas of `REGIONS` and knows nothing about drawing. It starts from a screenshot size, the found boxes and the old profile. Steps:
  - each area in turn, with its found box (or none);
  - keep the box, redraw it, or go back to the previous area;
  - after area 7, a review of all 7 boxes, where picking one goes to that area and then back to the review;
  - Save builds a new profile: the 7 boxes, `window_size` = screenshot size, `ui_scale`, landmark;
  - "save what you have" builds a profile from the confirmed boxes plus the old profile's boxes for the rest, with the new `window_size`.

**Done when:** `pytest tests/unit/test_calibration_steps.py` passes, with tests for keep, redraw, back, review jump, full save and partial save.

## Router notes

2026-10-22:
- C2.F3.K1 added `CalibrationProfile(landmark, regions, window_size=None, ui_scale=1.0)`. Regions are `Region` objects (`Region.from_list([x, y, w, h])`, `.to_list`).
- Owner decisions: Backspace on the first area does nothing. "Picking a box" in the review jumps to that area; after keep or redraw it returns to the review, not the next area. Partial save ("save what you have") uses only boxes the user confirmed (kept or redrawn) this run; an area with no confirmed box and no old box is left out. Full save is only allowed from the review, when all 7 areas have a box.
- Landmark is `(0, 0)` for now (the Stop button finder comes in C3.F1.K1); take it as a constructor argument with that default. `ui_scale` is a settable attribute, default 1.0.
- The tab (C2.F4.K2) will read from the class: the current area name, its box (or None), whether it is in the review, and all boxes. Keep those as plain attributes or properties.

## What changed and why

Added `CalibrationSteps` in `setup/calibration_steps.py`, a headless class that walks `REGIONS` with keep, redraw and back, then a review where `pick(name)` jumps to an area and keep or redraw returns to the review. `save()` (review only, all 7 boxes) and `save_partial()` (confirmed boxes plus old-profile boxes) build a `CalibrationProfile` with the screenshot size as `window_size`. Boxes are plain `Rect` tuples; conversion to `Region` happens at save. Tests cover keep, redraw, back, review jump, full and partial save.
