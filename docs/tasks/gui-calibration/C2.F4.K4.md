## What changed and why

- Esc now asks Save / Don't save / Cancel. Cancel keeps the tab as it was and gives the canvas focus back.
- `CalibrationSteps` has `next()` and `prev()`. They only move the index, so no box is changed or confirmed. They do nothing in the review or in a review jump, and stop at the ends. Left and right on the canvas call them.
- `AREA_HELP` in `calibration.py` holds one plain sentence for each of the 7 areas, shown under "3 of 7: name".
- The instructions sit in a raised bar above the picture, in normal text colour and bigger type, with the key help on its own line.
- After a save the idle view shows "Saved to <path>". It stays empty when the user chose Don't save or the write failed.
- Tests: `next`/`prev` in `test_calibration_steps.py`, and a check that every area has a sentence in `test_gui_calibration.py`.
